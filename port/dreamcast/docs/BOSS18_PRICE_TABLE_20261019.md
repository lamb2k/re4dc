## Basis and limits

The original console experiment is `EDEMO18-CONSOLE-20261019.md`: enemy-only simplified logic on edemo18 c138544c, NTSC Dreamcast, frames160–239 per segment. It supports near-linear draw scaling and the measured26% vertex/draw decrease for its g50 arm. It is **not a boss or whole-game benchmark**. For today's path the supplied140cycles/vertex at200MHz gives a rough vertex term `E(V) = 0.0007 × V ms` (0.70ms/1000). Neither the historical1.15ms/1000 census slope nor the new-path0.42 slope is used here. The1.3 correction applies only to SH4ZAM/new-path MODELLED numbers; it is not applied to today's console-fitted profiles. No future-path or reduction saving is called measured.

**V is a submitted-strip inventory proxy**, counting repeated index submissions in complete retained FE/v3 streams. The private receipt also records transformed meshlet records, strips, triangles, every source header hash and each individual stream. V is not unique positions and **is not a newly measured vertices-drawn/frame counter**. Camera clipping, source hidden parts, hand alternatives, alpha passes and active attachments can change actual work. For unqualified actors the table reports the complete-form inventory and explicitly leaves actual live-frame V unknown. Raw original PS2 counts are labelled separately; they are not native packet counts. Source alternatives and attachments are not silently summed into an asserted live frame.

`D` is exclusive native presentation from retained model-witnessed call-tree roots, excluding IRQ and separate preparation, logic/animation, Leon and auxiliary actors. It is MODELLED, not console timing. `E` is only the above vertex estimate of that draw term, not measured exclusive attribution. `U` means all unpriced disjoint remainder: boss preparation/gameplay, room/world, Leon/others, effects/HUD/audio/long tail and qualified source scheduling. A conditional `E+U` is not a completed projection or proof above33ms. Do not add overlapping animation/logic parents or divide aggregate drawing by actor count.

All priced diagnostic windows use **one logic tick per picture**. At steady30fps every tick is drawn, so per-drawn-tick and per-picture costs are equal; no half-rate multiplier or double-counted saving. Historical whole-window numbers below are on their named builds, not new full-fight or current-release projections. Slots1 historical b18srT evidence is not a substitute for current LEON_SKIN_SLOTS2 qualification.
