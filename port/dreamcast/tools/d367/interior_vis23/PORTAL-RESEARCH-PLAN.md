# House-interior portal research boundary

This is a source/data plan for owner review, not runtime integration or a working
house cut. Existing current-source camera proofs admit zero cuts. No target
build or guest is justified by this bounded cohort, and no milliseconds are
claimed from its geometry counts.

## Bounded current evidence

The 43 exact historical cross views, with explicit release LOD divisor 20 and
actual SG transform intervals, select 17059.35 corners per view. Composite owner
0x1e spans 13 placements and several buildings; its 1086.63 opaque corners are
an ownership ceiling, not an interior classification. Exact grid-edge components
show floor part 75 and inner-wall part 79 mixing houses within original meshlets
and clusters. Both parts' studied components are LOD0-only in these current data;
other parts and every unsupported source level remain fallback.

The physical seed house's floor/wall components, hypothetically hidden in every
view, save only 69.14 corners mean under full retained-child pricing. Every
unresolved boundary stays an opening. Seed and east-house opening bounds have
zero of 43 views with all openings outside. Sampled rays, recorded cameras and
an exact edge graph do not certify a continuous cell or source opacity.

## Minimal lossless data option

Prefer a dedicated retained-remainder variant over drawing the entire component
atlas when one named house is proven invisible:

1. Retain the original package and its default/fallback draw path byte for byte.
   For each affected original meshlet, retain complete source strips outside the
   proven house component. Cross-component and unclassified strips stay in that
   remainder. Preserve their original order, winding, twelve-byte corner values,
   palette references, material/UV policy, parent cluster, LOD choice and affine.
2. Compact only the remainder's referenced corners; do not bake world positions,
   simplify geometry, merge source owners or move a target between LODs. The host
   price reconstructs each remainder's retained ordered triangle stream and
   checks every corner byte and winding against the original source.
3. Draw that single remainder only after the named component is proven hidden.
   Otherwise draw the unchanged original meshlet. This preserves source strip
   order after removing only proven invisible triangles and needs at most one
   retained draw per affected original draw. Select at most one dedicated house
   variant at a time; simultaneous houses need a separately priced design.
4. For the seed floor/wall pair, raw additional remainder geometry/descriptors
   total 3538 bytes and optional SG records 448 bytes. These 3986 bytes exclude
   cell/source/target metadata, allocator costs and proof receipts. No package,
   sidecar format, loader or memory allocation has been implemented or qualified.
   Counts retain the original assets; they are not a peak-heap acceptance claim.

The generic component atlas remains a separate comparison. It costs 14386 raw
geometry bytes plus 4608 SG bytes and about 12.63 extra meshlet draws for the seed
case. Grouped atlas draw order is unqualified and should not become the chosen
runtime design on the basis of its source-reconstruction hash.

## Required proof before any runtime proposal

- Bind exact current mesh, physical policy/affines, original ownership, source
  material state, every target LOD, SG and source generation. IDs, component
  bounds and close-looking seams cannot substitute for a physical house proof.
- Construct the cell from actual opaque source triangles. Prove its oriented,
  non-self-intersecting enclosure and every claimed floor/roof/wall coverage
  continuously with existing numerical uncertainty. Do not invent planar fills
  between quantized source faces. Every uncovered seam, doorway, window, broken
  panel or unsupported LOD is an opening or an unconditional draw fallback.
- Classify complete target strips over every declared LOD as strictly interior.
  Keep exterior skins, mixed geometry and ambiguous owners. Cameras inside or
  crossing the cell and any unsupported eye state must draw normally.
- Only an outside eye with every possible opening conservatively outside the
  view can activate a remainder. Current padded boundary AABBs are overestimates
  of unresolved openings; they are not certified closed-shell data. Eight studied
  opening boxes require at most 40 plane tests before other cell/state work;
  there is no modeled or measured SH-4 cost for a proposed implementation.
- Retain the actual selected-affine identity, all numeric/material/opacity,
  source-prefix/emission and current state safeguards. Existing PVS rejects
  unsupported source LODs and target namespaces. Do not relax those guards to
  accommodate this research. A new contract/API/format requires owner review.
- Keep metadata room resident only, lend/free like r100, and price peak heap on
  the actual integration before staging. Actors and gameplay receive no new
  tests or changes. Default-off/PVS0 behavior and the ordinary recipe stay exact.

A useful, source-certified camera cohort must exist before any target timing,
console QA pair, pixel comparison or release gate is allocated. This study has
not established that cohort. Private frozen reports retain the exact geometry,
helper/profile hashes, historical camera identity and zero-cut limitations.
