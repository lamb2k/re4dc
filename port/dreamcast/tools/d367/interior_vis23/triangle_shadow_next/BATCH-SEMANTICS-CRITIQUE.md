## Current global exclusion versus proposed physical-part dispatch

Current scenery_chunk_select.inc chunk_ok requires !pvs_set in both ScenerySelector and MeshDraw. NativeStatic computes placement-start pvs_cur from can_cut, then assigns it to each matching part's selector/direct draw. Whenever that pointer is nonnull, every such later part loses chunk selection/replay/batched submission, including parts with no hidden bit. Current behavior is not candidate-specific. It also is not automatically the whole world: source-prefix and receipt guards can keep the pointer null before/at source placements, while queued sources may not yet have committed when the early pointer was captured.

The proposed fine mode must explicitly dispatch separately from v1. Leaving v1 unchanged preserves its existing global exclusion when a v1 set is active; that case cannot simultaneously be called unaffected-part batching. In a new typed fine-only mode, a valid internal current cell/set may be available for proof guards without copying its raw pointer into either context's legacy pvs_set field. Both selector branches (SELECT_ONLY1 and0) must bypass queueing only for a freshly qualified fine candidate. Simply attaching a v2/composite bitset through the existing assignments reproduces global de-batching, even if its legacy cluster prefix is all-visible. The typed mode/pointer authority and file certificate require later root-reviewed integration; this note supplies no API patch.

Authenticate candidate presence by CURRENT cell/set and PHYSICAL placement+field+part, not global part or meshlet alone. Reused geometry at another placement has another affine and source-prefix relation. The presence index means at least one eligible typed tuple in this physical part could be hidden by this exact authenticated set; bind or cold-derive it from the complete checked file. It is an optimization dispatch index, never authority to hide. Missing/invalid cells, absent tuples and any index uncertainty draw normally. Old coarse bits keep their meaning; a fine proof cannot hide its partly visible parent cluster.

Do cheap unchanged pass/placement filters before triggering an extra drain. Where source ordering permits, reject target placements at/before certificate.max_placement without a drain. Keep actual dynamic affine selection and its captured provenance: initial targets are independently static/unconditional and selected with pl.affine. The frozen tree's pvs_source_placement is only source-observation provenance, not general conditional-target admission. Do not requery fresh rest state to authorize a previously selected moved_affine or source hierarchy. Conditional target support remains a separate reviewed foundation.

For a potential fine candidate, drain older queued work in original order. After that drain, refresh the real frame/view/projection/viewport/near/far/FPSCR and cell identity, check source/room/package generation and selected target identity, and then require fresh complete accepted-prefix receipts. A stale placement-start pvs_cur is insufficient. If proof guards fail while ordinary package/pose references remain valid, current part can use the normal queue path after the now-empty drain. If room/package/binding generation changed, do not blindly queue the stale mvq, key or metadata: take the existing owner-safe abort/restart/fallback boundary. Visibility fail-open is not permission to dereference retired storage.

Only a successfully qualified candidate part goes direct. Resolve its LOD with the unchanged quality*far depth/error test and nonempty_lod BEFORE the typed let query, and check the actual tuple just before draw(let). Unaffected parts retain their normal SELECT_ONLY/REPLAY_LEAN/SUBMIT_BATCH path. Nevertheless, extra candidate drains split the surrounding unaffected runs and reduce their amortization; unchanged code path does NOT mean zero indirect cost.

## Queue, cache, pending UV and yield obligations

Pinned queue capacity is48 ChunkPart contexts and320 eight-byte Pick records. Capacity admission reserves the whole authored part meshlet count before chunk_select, not just its eventual selected picks. A queued snapshot includes mvq, material key, effective far/LOD scale, part index, cull, marked vertex-alpha state and source placement provenance; optional UV and sky members are separately gated. Preserve both capacity branches, zero-pick contexts, source/pass order and all normal/abort exits. No new Pick field, reordering or selection-time expected-receipt cut is proposed.

chunk_flush always has a pending-cull scope and restores the sky header when enabled. Its pending UV scope and queued UV copies exist only with SCENERY_UV_SNAPSHOT1. The pinned R6 profile is UV_SNAPSHOT0 and TRANSITION_OWNER0; its saved work cannot certify a later GC source-UV composition. A new on-demand drain should occur BEFORE preparing/mutating the current part's source UV/cull/key/sky state, then prepare that pending state once afterward under a stable binding. If ordering cannot be preserved, a separately reviewed exact pending-state save/restore is necessary; do not assume UV restoration exists with the knob0, silently flip the recipe, or invoke a stateful UV hook twice. Future owner-enabled controls must exercise two queued parts with different UVs and a pending candidate's different UV on normal, guard-failure and abort paths.

REPLAY_LEAN creates one reusable emitter per flush; chunk_restart clears packet/cache/pending state, and bind/borrow reacquire the actual packet/cache/constants. Fragmentation can change packet reuse, header binding, cache prime, gather and submission-lock amortization. Existing SUBMIT_BATCH is scoped to synchronous replay and closes on abort; candidate direct draws must not retain that lock or inherit stale scratch/cache pointers. No current WORLD_SCRATCH loan is credited to new PVS memory.

A fresh can_cut call by itself DOES NOT refresh numerical key state. In the pinned runtime it performs source/scene/receipt checks, while key/FPSCR refresh happens in frame_guard, normally through pvs::frame. Binding, queue replay or callbacks that can yield/change a frame, projection, viewport, FPSCR or generation therefore require the appropriate real guard/cell refresh at the actual decision. If the refreshed cell differs, the authenticated candidate-presence decision must be recomputed, not reused from the old cell. Failed/revoked state draws normally only with still-valid ordinary references. Any later visible meshlet's bind can invalidate the next query; never hoist a true fine-cut guard across all lets of a part.

Source panels still need actual successful observe/commit under the correct cull, clip, material/header and source affine. A queue containing the panel is not a receipt, a failed/partial draw cannot complete it, and a generation/key change clears it. Require target placement strictly after every source panel's max placement and never target a required source panel or create cyclic dependencies. Meshlet granularity does not relax the single-LOD source guard or current renderer/raster obligations.

Let G be actual guarded global-fallback part visits, C be authenticated candidate-presence visits and D the freshly qualified direct subset. The global design loses batching on G. The candidate design loses direct-path amortization on D and can add at most one requested drain per C visit; empty or already-required drains add no new flush, while every extra nonempty drain can fragment an otherwise batched surrounding run. Guard failure can still incur a drain and save zero work. Offscreen/unselected potential tuples can likewise incur a metadata-triggered drain unless unchanged earlier filters avoid it. Counts of C,D and extra nonempty drains are not available from saved cluster weights.

Price matched event buckets: candidate-index and fresh-guard lookup; extra actual nonempty flush/batch-open-close/packet binding; changed direct-part setup and lost cache/gather/prime amortization; selected let transforms/gather/strips/submission actually removed. Avoid double-counting shared overhead between those buckets. Dense188B sets fitting at2m does not establish free heap or cheap dispatch; K22 sparse has higher fixed maps and is not the chosen cohort. No ms conversion or PVS speed claim follows from triangle/corner savings alone.

Before any implementation approval, the future matched census needs: reason-coded capacity/pass/direct/fine drains; queued contexts/picks and zero-pick counts per drain; successful batch-open/close and aborts; actual material binds, cache borrows/prime/gather work; current physical-part candidate/direct/fallback visits; effective LOD/SG-restricted per-let corners counted once; frame/view/FPSCR/source/UV/room-generation invalidations; and actual elapsed rendering/setup buckets. These are explicitly UNKNOWN now. Root has allocated no runtime instrumentation or guest for this note.
