## What a logical witness and its aliases mean

A logical single-triangle witness fixes one oriented physical triangle in one placement/field/mesh/part/cluster, under the exact current grid decode and physical affine. A per-LOD alias identifies an actual meshlet and parity-correct source index triple in that SAME owner/material cohort. All aliases must reconstruct that exact oriented geometry; cyclic triangle rotation preserves orientation, reversal does not. Comparing exact quantized geometry after collapsed zero-step coordinates is not a seam weld, fuzzy tolerance or permission to pair foreign faces.

Store/authenticate every effective-level membership, including empty-LOD fallthrough. Every possible effective source level must have at least one alias for the logical witness, or the all-LOD source contract fails open. Do not infer coverage from current selected frames. A changed package, IDs, physical affine/material/part, nonempty policy or alias membership invalidates the group. Existing all-LOD numeric world_abs must cover absolute operands for ALL aliases; geometry equality after cancellation is insufficient for that envelope.

Actual current Panel can mechanically represent one triangle by duplicating its exact triple. panel_sent still requires that actual triple in an unchanged parity-correct source strip, full meshlet cache validity, positive reciprocal, depth-plane/oversized-strip rejection and strong actual projected area/cull. The source placement must be the captured original selected affine, never moved/hidden/deformed/part-driven state or queued expectation. Observe alone is pending; commit must successfully finish the matching placement/part with real opaque upload/header state and unchanged epoch/key. Frustum/far/near/SG rejection or a partial SG pick supplies no source witness even if that alias is authored in the selected LOD. Maintain current source-prefix ordering, scene/frame/room/generation/view/projection/FPSCR invalidation and target source-intact checks.

## Why the current flat mask cannot express alternatives

Current kMaxPanels is16, not32. npanels indexes16 flat descriptor bits. Receipts.complete requires ALL npanels bits, and observe/commit map those exact descriptor bits by placement/part/meshlet. Flattening all LOD aliases into npanels would require all effective LODs to emit in one epoch, although the renderer normally chooses one. Counting just one alias ignores other effective LODs. Neither is a valid implementation.

Two later schema choices, neither approved:

1. Keep at most16 FLAT alias descriptors and existing pending alias bits, but add explicitly authenticated logical-group alternative masks. Completion becomes AND over groups of OR over their committed aliases. A single-triangle group with2/3/4 aliases consumes2/3/4 descriptor slots; at most8/5/4 such groups fit16. Group masks must be nonempty, in range, semantically equivalent and owner/material consistent. Old certificates retain their old AND meaning through explicit version/capability dispatch.
2. Keep at most16 LOGICAL witness bits, scan validated alias descriptors separately, and map successful actual alias observations/commits onto their logical group. This can separate alias storage from mask width, but requires reviewed certificate/observer/commit ownership semantics, explicit bounded alias count, exact per-alias material and epoch provenance, storage and hot lookup budgets. Current pending/commit code cannot simply reinterpret the bits. No new count/cap is chosen here.

Current Panel can hold two actual oriented triangles; pairing two semantic faces into one alias panel requires both in the same qualifying meshlet with appropriate real shared-index topology in EVERY alias variant. The single-triangle census does not establish those pairings or provide a compressed panel budget. Raising kMaxPanels to32 is not a shortcut: current `(1U<<n)-1U` expressions have a32-bit shift boundary and need separate reviewed semantics. This design keeps16 authoritative.

The observer currently receives placement, part and meshlet, but not cluster/effective LOD. A future contract must explicitly decide whether cold exact alias membership plus actual identical meshlet geometry suffices, or whether actual cluster/effective-LOD provenance must be propagated. Do not guess a current level from a saved roster. Any signature/storage expansion belongs to root review, not this audit.

## Coverage proof and topology remain separate

For an individual triangle occluding a complete target box, one accepted equivalent actual alias can witness the SAME strict continuous geometric triangle proof. That still requires actual transform/selector uncertainty, near/far/cull/raster/depth and numeric/material/source-prefix receipts; exact authored equality does not prove those.

For a two-triangle or larger patch, an AND of independently satisfied triangle groups is not automatically a valid seam/raster witness. The all-LOD triangle intersection does not establish matching real shared cache indices, kernel, transform or unchanged clip construction across those triangles. It also does not prevent receipts from mixing LOD variants in repeated draws within one otherwise unchanged epoch. A conservative patch contract should qualify an ENTIRE coherent actual indexed panel/patch variant first, then OR over complete LOD variants. Source geometry copying or coordinate-equal independent corners must never invent a seam. Each source LOD must preserve the required real topology, or that patch remains unsupported.

Required future controls include missing/reversed aliases at one effective level, empty fallback, changed UV/color/material/alpha/cull, different affine or placement reuse, wrong part/meshlet/triple, no actual selected emission, partial/clipped/oversized/failed source, stale keys/epochs, cross-owner commit, alias bit/group confusion, mixed LOD patch receipts, out-of-range/overlapping masks and cap boundary. No such runtime contract has been implemented or admitted here. The current completed one-level discovery remains unchanged, renderer cuts0 and MODELLED ms unavailable.
