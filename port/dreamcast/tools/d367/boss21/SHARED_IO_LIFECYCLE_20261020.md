Generic main+blend, held shapes and separately updated enemy weapons can add recurring streams even when each evaluator holds only one short lease. The known Ashley and Krauser examples remain specific proofs; the remaining union is not a concurrency proof.

## Bounded motion-resume component prepared for integration

[ResumeJournal](../../../game/platform/motion_resume_journal.hpp) is metadata only:424B fixed state,144B ticket result, zero allocations/pins. It records only successful source acquires in the current/previous source frame, intersects that footprint with actual **movie-owned heap-key evictions**, and tags tickets by binding generation. Bind/unbind clears that slot's history, including archive-address reuse. Ordinary pressure/OOM/weapon eviction and bind-preloaded-but-unused keys do not become tickets. Held/stale/retiring storage must be skipped by the caller. Source frame callback already exists as `re4dc_fixture_source_frame()`.

The component's actual header passes ASan/UBSan tests for recent/stale footprints, two source frames, frame wrap, unrelated keys, four-binding/255-entry bounds, nested begin rejection, idempotent close, cancel, rebind during movie and stale tickets. It is **not called by the runtime yet**: Claude motion21 currently owns native_motion.cpp/game30.mk integration, and root owns weapon/read.cpp. No motion restoration fix or target/guest acceptance is claimed from this component.

Runtime hookup must be a separate default-off change after ownership coordination: mark successful acquire; start/record movie eviction; invalidate tickets on every binding retirement/dead-table drop; after buffers free, validate generation/heap4/hold/retirement and preflight the entire missing group against the unchanged cap and allocation overhead. Restoration must not evict other useful keys, spill v3, change pointers, bypass read identity, or synchronously replay all old hot flags. A failed budget/fragmentation check leaves explicit unresolved cold demand rather than claiming closure. A stepped movie and changed post-event state require separate coverage: the recent pre-boundary group does not predict a newly chosen attack.

QA owns full-program/normal-input validation. The original post-s20 QA12 reproduction is valid; earlier hidden pre-s03 controls must not be reused as ambush acceptance. Console farm, whole fights and post-return no-read closure remain open.
