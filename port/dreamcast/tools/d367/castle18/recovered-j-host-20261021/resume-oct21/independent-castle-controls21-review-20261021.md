## Findings from the actual frozen source

- `src/game/pl_wep.cpp:1003-1014`: `repCtr` increments to 7 while either main stick axis is nonzero and resets to zero when both are neutral. RT alone does not retain acceleration. The yaw accumulator uses `-stickX * repCtr * PI / (10*200*20)`, with denominator 40000, and body yaw beyond the weapon's waist clamp additionally uses the weapon speed. The default weapon profile has `spd=spd2=1`, waist limits +0.20943952 / -0.167551616 radians. These are source control equations, not an achieved turn or timing measurement.
- `pl_wep.cpp:1016-1048`: system flag bit 31 controls inversion. With bit 31 clear, positive main-stick Y raises source pitch blend; with it set, the sign reverses. The 249 snapshot has flags `0x02000000`, so it is noninverted. `m3r[0]` and `[1]` are blend values, not a measurement of the animated muzzle angle. Positive blend changes are scaled by 0.8; the blend clamps to [-1,1].
- `pl_wep.cpp:1112-1146`: sway changes pitch/yaw around the aim center. `PlSetLockPitch` at 1250-1288 initializes the blend when aiming starts, from the camera pitch when system bit 29 is clear, doubling positive camera pitch. Bit 29 is clear in the inspected state. Recompute actual aim AFTER entering RT; pre-RT pitch cannot be carried forward as a settled weapon angle.
- `src/game/pl_class.cpp:577-619`: aim is selected before quickturn in `actionSelect`. `keyConfigTypeA` at 1000-1012 creates quickturn trigger 0x100 from Back plus a two-tick B trigger window. Established Back acknowledgement before B, with RT released, is appropriate. `player.cpp:702-756` runs the authored quickturn motion; do not infer completion from the three-tick B hold alone. Poll ordinary routine and yaw.
- Ordinary standing J/L prealignment uses existing turn motion, selected only from idle by `actionSelect`; `player.cpp:669-698` retains it while the side key is held. The numeric walk-turn constant is not a qualification of standing turn speed. Use actual body yaw feedback and release/settle before RT.
- `src/game/objWep.cpp:362-408`: weapon number 2 muzzle offset is (228,-24,29), transformed by weapon part 0. The ray follows its negative X axis. The existing controller's transformed offset and `[-m[0],-m[4],-m[8]]` direction match this source. Camera/body bearing alone is not a firing predicate.
- `src/game/pad.cpp:129-153` and `include/joy.h`: source `Key.on` direction bits are translated from raw Joy stick/D-pad bits. `Joy[0].on & 0xf` denotes raw D-pad inputs, while main stick direction bits are separate. Log both raw stick values and translated Key state when proving a delivered input. Do not substitute host-key events for source acknowledgement.
