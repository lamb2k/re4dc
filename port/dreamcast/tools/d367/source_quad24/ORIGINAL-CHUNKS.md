## Scope and sufficient composition

The proposed next experiment uses the SAME 25 physical lets, complete 889 original oriented triangle occurrences, unchanged full physicalPanels and SAME 75 authenticated hut647 eye domains. A different triangle may choose a different fullPanel; one entire original triangle and entire eye domain always use one fixed assignment. There is no per-vertex, per-eye or within-triangle source switching. There are no newly rendered vertices/triangles or clipped target domains.

`evaluate` authenticates the caller's complete inventory digest before predicates, reserves the full original-count × candidate-count case, then visits EVERY original, including those after an unknown original. It tries an explicitly supplied fixed candidate roster in order. It stores either the full assignment or an explicit unproved draw reason at exactly that original ordinal. It then performs a complete final pass: the covered/draw vectors must be a disjoint exhaustive bijection of the ORIGINAL inventory, and EVERY covered original is revalidated by all 128 signed-volume upper tests of `cover_triangle` over its entire eye box. Unproved/degenerate inventory, missing/wrong/wound/affine-rebound parents, counter/allocation/refusal/final failure returns UNKNOWN; caller retains ALL originals for a refused result. A valid mixed partition retains every unproved original draw. No new subset hash is passed off as the old wholelet hash, and `cover_assigned` is not called with a pruned inventory.

A selected result may use at most 16 fullPanel dependencies. `cell_dependencies` additionally takes the AND union across ALL included physical lets for one identical numeric whole-eye domain, refusing duplicate physical lets, aliases, different boxes or a global union above 16. Per-inventory source bindings remain intact; the future caller must authenticate that the numeric boxes also have the same original camera/cell provenance. A per-let positive is not a substitute for this union check. An over-limit union leaves that entire coverage-cell proposal unqualified/drawn; no silent panel trimming or partial cell positive.

`Validated` means nominal public exact-real composition, NOT authenticated asset evidence or runtime admission. Serialized/caller-created instances do not replace rerunning validation. `cell_dependencies` and `groups` check final-result input hashes, complete vectors and assignment identity but are not independent geometric re-provers; consume them only from `evaluate`/`validate` in the same trusted process. Material IDs/hashes supplied by a caller are assertions, not new provenance evidence.

## Three-domain grouping

After complete results exist for all three ordered domains (future adapter order: 2000, 500, 250 mm), `groups` groups ORIGINAL occurrences with the same three-bit nominal coverage signature inside ONE unchanged original physical let/inventory and one explicitly supplied material scope. The domains are nested whole eye boxes around the same authenticated hut647 view, NOT three independent played views, nor disjoint grid cells. Bit0 means the first supplied domain and a zero bit always retains the draw in that domain. Mask0 is always drawn. All occurrences remain exactly once, with original oriented index triples, exact vertices and affine tokens; no welding across parent material, placement or effective LOD. Unknown results cannot become hidden bits. A missing domain/material record blocks grouping. Current saved metadata has no authenticated material-run roster, so actual grouping remains HELD.

Disconnected original triangles may share a signature. That does not make a geometric patch or authorize triangle-strip joining. Future data construction would need exact strip winding/restart/degenerate and material-run authentication and lossless original occurrence correspondence first. No construction is performed here.
